This ongoing research project develops η-regularisation as part of a broader programme toward a generalised theory of regularisation in quantum field theory. The central aim is to identify and classify new admissible classes of η regulators, understand the physical principles that constrain them, and determine when finite parts of divergent quantities are uniquely fixed by symmetry, analyticity, unitarity, or deeper ultraviolet structure. A distinctive feature of the project is its attempt to build a bridge between fundamental physics and foundational mathematics, e.g., discrete mathematics and analytic number theory. Te η framework provides a way to reinterpret regularisation not merely as a technical prescription, but as a structured problem about the emergence of finite, physically meaningful quantities from formally divergent expressions. Current directions include the classification of new η regulator classes in number theory, including in the context of modular forms, and with application in perturbative quantum field theory. --- layout: page title: project description: a project with a background image img: /assets/img/12.jpg ---
