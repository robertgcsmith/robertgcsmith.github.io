# Hi, I'm [Robert]({{ '/' | relative_url }}).

I'm a theoretical physicist with a PhD, now working as a machine learning researcher. My interests span machine learning theory, algorithmic foundations and mathematically grounded methods for scientific discovery, particularly generative modelling, active learning, reinforcement learning, sequential decision-making, inverse problems and simulation-based inference.

What motivates me is how we learn, infer, simulate, represent and make decisions about complex scientific systems when data are limited, experiments are expensive, uncertainty matters and mechanistic structure is available.

*The Stochastic Ledger* is my machine learning research blog: thoughts and notes on machine learning, quantitative modelling and advanced mathematical methods, with applications in computational biology and physics.
